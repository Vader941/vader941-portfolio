# Portfolio Working Guidelines

## Purpose
This portfolio presents Nathan Able for Business Systems Analyst roles,
with implementation work as a secondary direction.

## Voice and factual accuracy
- Preserve Nathan's natural voice in all visitor-facing text.
- Use approved copy exactly. Do not rewrite it unless asked.
- Avoid generic corporate language, inflated claims, and overly polished slogans.
- Do not invent accomplishments, credentials, metrics, or responsibilities.
- Clearly distinguish previous employment, current work, and ongoing projects.
- Flag meaningful factual ambiguity without demanding unnecessary precision
  for older experiences recalled from memory.

## Design and decisions
- Explain the purpose and tradeoff of proposed content or design changes.
- Preserve the professional/cyborg split portrait unless Nathan requests otherwise.
- Maintain responsive layouts, accessible contrast, keyboard navigation,
  and visible focus indicators.
- Keep solutions maintainable and avoid unnecessary dependencies.

## Workflow
- Work page by page within the requested scope.
- Inspect existing HTML, CSS, and JavaScript before editing.
- Do not commit, push, or deploy unless explicitly requested.
- Report files changed, checks performed, and any checks requiring manual review.
